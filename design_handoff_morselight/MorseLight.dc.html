What changed
One object per job. The old screen stacked four outlined boxes with no hierarchy. Now the torch is the only large target on Send, and the text, morse and speed sit under it in reading order. Tappable things look tappable. Pill buttons, filled accent for the one primary action, outlined secondaries. The read-only morse and decoded output are sunken cards, never bordered boxes that look like fields. Live transmit feedback. The torch fills with accent, glows, and the morse string highlights the symbol currently going out. Signal and SOS demoted, not removed. Both were solid colour blocks competing with Start. They are now soft-tinted buttons flanking the one filled primary — Signal fires the attention prosign before a message, SOS the distress group. Nothing scrolls. Every screen fits the phone, with the hold target sitting low where the thumb is. Camera tuning moved out of the viewfinder. Sliders no longer overlay the preview; they sit in a Tuning card beneath it.
